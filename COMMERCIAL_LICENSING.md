# Licensing and commercial use

The name of this file is kept because other documents link to it; it no longer
describes a separate commercial licence, because `wise-pm` needs none.

`wise-pm` (everything under `packages/wise-pm/`, in this version and every
earlier one) is distributed under the [MIT licence](LICENSE). It may be used,
including commercially, without separate permission; the MIT terms ask only that
the copyright and permission notice are kept in copies and substantial portions.
Its dependencies, including the optional `pm4py`, keep their own licences.

A package added to this workspace later may carry another licence. Its own
folder and its package metadata will state that licence; none is here today.
Other parts of WISE, such as the map library and the workbench, live in their
own repositories under their own licences.

Questions about licensing go to Urszula Jessen, the copyright holder, through
the [issue tracker](https://github.com/feelfine1977/wise-pm/issues) or her
GitHub profile [feelfine1977](https://github.com/feelfine1977).
