retained MIT material together with prospective PolyForm-covered additions.
It does not mean that either licence can be chosen for the entire package,
or impose PolyForm restrictions on use of the retained MIT material alone.

## Commercial permission and process.science

The intended commercial route is separate written permission from the relevant
rights holder(s) for uses of the PolyForm-covered additions outside that
licence's permitted purposes. The exact PolyForm terms, including their
organisation provisions, determine what is already permitted.

Commercialisation through process.science requires identification of the actual
contracting legal entity and documented authority covering the relevant code,
contributions and permitted sublicensing. This document neither asserts that
process.science owns those rights nor grants it or anyone else an automatic
commercial exception. No alternative commercial licence is supplied here.

Before issuing a commercial licence, establish the necessary contributor,
employer, university and other rights, and settle its product, hosting,
redistribution, affiliate, customer and sublicensing scope. Submission under
PolyForm alone does not provide a separate commercial relicensing grant.

## Third-party material and limits

Dependencies and separately licensed material retain their own terms and
attributions. A commercial licence for first-party additions cannot waive
third-party requirements. In particular, optional PM4Py integration requires
review of its applicable AGPL or separate commercial terms for the actual
combination and distribution. Citation of a paper does not confer rights to
copy someone else's code, documents or assets.

This policy grants no ownership of public ideas, mathematical principles or
algorithms and promises no exclusion of any named company. It does not modify
the licence text or waive statutory rights.
